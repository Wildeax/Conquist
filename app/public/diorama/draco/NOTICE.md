# Draco decoder

`draco_wasm_wrapper.js` and `draco_decoder.wasm` are the unmodified glTF
decoders distributed with Three.js 0.186.0 in `examples/jsm/libs/draco/gltf/`.
They originate from Google's Draco project, licensed under Apache 2.0.
The license is included in `LICENSE`.

Upstream: https://github.com/google/draco
Packaged decoder documentation: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r186/examples/jsm/libs/draco

The models in the neighboring `v1` directory were authored for Conquist using
the Blender scripts in `art/blender`. These decoder notices do not change the
project's license for its own assets.
